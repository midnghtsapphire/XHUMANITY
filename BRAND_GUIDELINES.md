# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Position

XHUMANITY stands for measurable climate action, transparent reporting, and credible delivery.

## Voice

- Clear
- Evidence-based
- Urgent but factual
- Action-oriented

## Messaging Rules

1. Always include the mission in communications:
   - “Reduce global carbon emissions by 1.5 trillion kg CO2e by 2035.”
2. Never present projections as achieved outcomes.
3. Pair all claims with data source and time period.

## Visual Direction

- Primary palette: deep graphite, electric teal, clean white
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast minimum
- Charts: always show baseline, target, and variance

## Claim Safety

- Use “verified reduction” only for externally auditable numbers.
- Use “estimated reduction” for model-based forward projections.
